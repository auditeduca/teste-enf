About the MMSE

Creation Year: 1975

Authors: Marshal F. Folstein, Susan E. Folstein, and Paul R. McHugh

Applied Area: Geriatrics, Neurology, Psychiatry, and Mental Health

Who uses it: Doctors, neuropsychologists, nurses, psychologists, and therapists.

Cutoff Points

Illiterate Cutoff <= 20
1 to 11 years of education Cutoff <= 24
More than 11 years Cutoff <= 28

Score below cutoff suggests possible cognitive deficit and indicates in-depth assessment.
